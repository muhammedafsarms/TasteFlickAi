# **App Name**: TasteFlick

## Core Features:

- The Flavor Flick: A swiping interface to rapidly browse through suggested recipes, where users can 'flick' right to save and left to dismiss.
- Smart Pantry Alchemist: An AI tool that reasons through your available pantry ingredients to generate gourmet-style recipes specifically tailored to your dietary constraints.
- Aura Analysis: AI-driven estimation of nutritional density and caloric breakdown for every generated recipe to help users maintain balance.
- Visual Pantry Builder: An intuitive grid layout to quickly tap and add ingredients you have on hand.
- One-Tap Grocery Generator: Instantly compiles missing ingredients from saved recipes into a checklist-ready shopping list.
- Taste Vault: A clean, high-fidelity gallery for viewing and editing saved recipe cards with full instructions.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Vibrant Saffron (#D17F14) used for primary buttons and interactive flick elements, evoking warmth and flavor.
- Background color: Soft Linen (#FAF8F5), a warm off-white that provides a sophisticated, paper-like texture for content-heavy cards.
- Accent color: Deep Paprika (#AD1C1C), used for contrast against the saffron and for secondary highlighting and error states.
- A refined pairing of 'Playfair' (serif) for headlines and recipe titles, and 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for body text and ingredient lists, creating an editorial feel.
- Fine-line vector icons with slight variations in stroke weight to reflect a handcrafted, artisanal kitchen aesthetic.
- Magazine-inspired whitespace with prominent food photography; swipe cards use an layered Z-axis stack for the flicking interaction.
- Buttery smooth CSS transitions for swipe-left/right interactions and micro-interactions for expanding recipe steps.
